About

A paper atlas for cycle tours. Days are computed from effort, not stored as an itinerary.

This is Your Bike Route Planners by CT. Pick a country, then a trip. How many days you get follows the daily effort you set — the catalogue is not a fixed timetable.

Every stretch carries elevation, shops, beds, baths, drinking water, stations and sights. Forks let you take a coast, a pass, or a town. Export GPX or KML, or print the sheet.

Photographs are Wikimedia Commons copies kept in this repository. Towns on a ride — Japan’s cape-to-cape especially — use those place faces. The atlas does not fetch other sites for pictures.

Four countries:

  • Britain by bike: 23 trips. Land’s End to Edinburgh on the west, or via London, then the London and Ealing days.
  • Japan by bike: 29 trips. Shimanami, Biwaichi, Fuji-ichi, Tokapuchi 400, and 3,550 km cape Sōya to cape Sata.
  • Switzerland by bike: 30 trips. National routes 1–9 and 99, pass days, lake loops, and the Swiss leg of EuroVelo 6.
  • Across Spain: 8 trips. Camino Francés, Norte, Invierno, and Cap de Creus to Fisterra.
