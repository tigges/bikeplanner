About

This webpage is Your Bike Route Planners by CT, an open web platform that helps cyclists plan customized trips across Britain, Japan, Switzerland, and Spain. Rather than using a fixed schedule, it dynamically calculates trip duration based on your intended daily effort.

The platform features:

  • Britain by bike: 23 trips, including a 1,133 km west crossing and a 1,372 km route via London.
  • Japan by bike: 29 trips, featuring 15 national routes and a 3,550 km cape-to-cape route.
  • Switzerland by bike: 30 trips, including national routes 1–9 and 99, mountain passes, lake loops, and EuroVelo 6.
  • Across Spain: 8 trips, covering 4 distinct caminos spanning 1,435 km east-to-west.

Every listed route automatically integrates critical details like elevation, local shops, accommodations (beds), and sights along each stretch.
